We build language software for people learning the language of their family. Our first app is Idemo — a guided journey across Bosnia, from your first zdravo to dinner at the family table.

Idemo — Learn Bosnian

Idemo is a journey across Bosnia, not a streak machine. You travel with Edo, an American learning the language of his family, alongside his friends Amila and Mirko — and teaching modules build toward language you can genuinely use with a real person.

A journey with a finish line

Five real cities — Sarajevo, Mostar, Tuzla, Banja Luka and Bihać — a story you move through, and a clear end.

Real conversations

You answer, you ask back, you say things that are true. You never simply echo a line back.

Verb-first

Teaching modules center on verbs you will really use — present, past and future — then put them into conversations that matter.

Hear everything

Natural, carefully produced audio for lessons, reference words, and a new phrase every day.

A reference in your pocket

Verbs in every tense, plus numbers, family words, time and greetings — each a tap away from hearing it.

Remember what you learn

Review brings back exactly what you are about to forget, and regular reviews bring the cast back together to use everything you have learned.

About the company

AJ Digital Services LLC is a software company registered in the State of Florida, United States. We design and publish mobile applications, beginning with language learning.

We build a small number of products and look after them properly. Idemo exists because the Bosnian language deserves teaching material as considered as the language itself — and because the people learning it are usually learning it for someone they love.

We collect only the information an app needs in order to work. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it beyond the service providers required to operate and distribute our software. Our privacy policy is linked below.
